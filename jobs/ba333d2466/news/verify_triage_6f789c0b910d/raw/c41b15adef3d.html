BYD now has 1.02 million employees – more than Toyota, GM, Ford and Tesla combined
Thursday, 29 October 2026, 02:43

Photo source: Electrek

BYD says its workforce has topped 1.02 million, about 170,000 more people than Toyota, GM, Ford and Tesla employ combined. Most work in China, and the total includes the company’s electronics, battery and other businesses as well as its car operations.

BYD chairman Wang Chuanfu gave the latest headcount at the China Society of Automotive Engineers’ 33rd annual conference on Wednesday. He said about 960,000 employees are in China and 60,000 abroad, with up to 120,000 working as R&D engineers on battery cells, motors, electronic controls and semiconductors.

This is reported by Electrek.

The figure is roughly 150,000 higher than BYD’s reported 869,600 employees at the end of 2025. Data compiled by S&P Global Market Intelligence put the company at 911,900 employees at the end of June, implying an increase of more than 100,000 in about four months.

BYD’s workforce has grown and contracted sharply in recent years. The company had about 570,100 employees at the end of 2022 and 703,500 at the end of 2023. It passed 900,000 in September 2024, when it also claimed the largest R&D workforce of any automaker, with more than 110,000 engineers. BYD ended 2024 with 968,900 employees, then cut about 99,000 by the end of 2025, mostly from frontline production jobs.

Wang credited vertical integration for BYD’s scale, saying the company keeps batteries, chips and motors in-house. Founded in 1994 as a battery maker, BYD has become the world’s top-selling new energy vehicle brand for several years running. Wang described the company’s rise as a reflection of China’s auto industry moving from follower to leader.

BYD’s headcount is larger than that of other major automakers. Volkswagen Group, including its Chinese joint ventures, reported 662,942 employees at the end of 2025. Toyota had 390,927 as of March 31, 2026, while Stellantis reported 258,668 at the end of 2025. Ford ended 2025 with 169,000 employees and GM with 156,000. Tesla’s latest 10-K reported 134,785 employees worldwide. Toyota, GM, Ford and Tesla together had about 851,000.

Employee totals do not directly measure output. BYD sold about 4.6 million vehicles in 2025, or 5.3 per year-end employee. Tesla delivered 1,636,129 vehicles, about 12 per employee, and Volkswagen Group produced a ratio of about 13.5. Toyota sold 11.3 million vehicles, including Daihatsu and Hino, or roughly 29 per employee.

Those comparisons do not account for the breadth of BYD’s businesses. Its headcount includes BYD Electronic, a majority-owned contract manufacturer that assembles devices for other brands and reported 165,000 employees at the end of 2025. It also includes the battery business, which supplies other automakers, as well as operations in chips, motors, energy storage and rail transit. The company’s vertical integration is a major reason its workforce is so large.

BYD sold 3,131,576 vehicles in the first nine months of 2026, down 3.9% year over year. September was its best month of the year, with 463,561 sales, but the company has faced a price war in China and lost ground to competitors including Geely and Leapmotor. First-half revenue was 344.8 billion yuan and net profit was 12.3 billion yuan, both lower year over year.

International sales reached 1,342,960 vehicles through September, up 92.7% year over year and about 43% of BYD’s total volume. The company also again surpassed Tesla in pure electric vehicle deliveries in the second quarter. Despite that overseas growth, only about 60,000 of BYD’s 1.02 million employees work outside China – around 6%. Its factories in Thailand, Brazil and Hungary are coming online as tariffs in Europe, Brazil, Mexico and elsewhere push the company toward local production.

The employee count alone is not a clear measure of strength. BYD’s integrated model helps it sell the Seagull for around $10,000 in China while still making money, but it relies on a large workforce and relatively cheap, plentiful factory labor in China. As the company expands overseas, it remains to be seen whether local factories can deliver the same model. The scale of its R&D workforce – up to 120,000 people, nearly as many as Tesla’s total headcount – is another notable part of the comparison.

About 57 thousand civilians remain in the area of active fighting in Donetsk region
Thursday, 29 October 2026, 02:42

Photo source: naslidky rosiiskykh udariv po Kramatorsku, iliustratyvne foto: State Emergency Service of Ukraine

Since October, more than 8 thousand civilians have left the region. Overall, since February 2022, about 1 million 472.4 thousand people have evacuated from the government-controlled part of Donetsk region.

About 57 thousand people, including more than 1.7 thousand children, remain in communities in Donetsk region designated as areas of active hostilities. This concerns 21 communities.

This is reported by Ukrinform.

Dmytro Petlin, a representative of the Donetsk Regional Military Administration, said at an online briefing, Ukrinform reports. According to him, about 67.6 thousand people remain in the part of the region under Ukrainian government control.

“The total number of people currently counted in our territory of the region under Ukrainian government control is about 67.6 thousand people. In the communities designated as areas of active hostilities – and there are 21 communities – about 57 thousand people remain”

– Dmytro Petlin

According to local authorities, more than 8 thousand civilians have left Donetsk region since October. Since February 2022, about 1 million 472.4 thousand people have evacuated from their permanent residences in the government-controlled part of the region, including 217 thousand children.

Earlier, it was reported that 1746 children from 1580 families remained in settlements where the mandatory evacuation of children with their parents had been announced.

On October 22, the Donetsk Regional Military Administration extended the mandatory forced evacuation of families with children to all settlements in the Kramatorsk and Sloviansk territorial communities of Kramatorsk district.
