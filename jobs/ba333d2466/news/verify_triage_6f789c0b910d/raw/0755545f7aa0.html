SK On’s Iváncsa Battery Plant in Hungary Receives Ford Q1 Certification

Published: Oct 07, 2026 12:01 (GMT+8)
Share
Share
Save

SK On announced on October 7 that its Iváncsa battery plant in Hungary had received Ford’s Q1 certification, the automaker’s highest-level supplier quality certification. The award ceremony took place at the plant on October 6, local time.

The Iváncsa plant began mass production in the second quarter of 2024 and has an annual production capacity of 30 GWh. Including the 7.5 GWh Komárom Plant 1 and 10 GWh Komárom Plant 2, SK On’s total annual production capacity in Hungary is 47.5 GWh.

[Lopal Tech: Plans to Invest RMB 942 Million to Build 150,000 t/y Battery-Grade Iron Phosphate Project]
Lopal Tech announced that its second-tier controlled subsidiary, Shandong Liyuan, plans to invest in and construct the “Shandong Liyuan LP-15 Project” in the Juancheng County Chemical Industry Park, Shandong Province. The project has a total planned construction of a 150,000 t/y battery-grade iron phosphate production line, with a total investment of approximately RMB 942 million. The investment aims to achieve internal supporting of iron phosphate raw materials, stabilize the supply chain, reduce procurement costs, and ensure the implementation of lithium iron phosphate cathode material production capacity. The project will be funded by proceeds from the company’s 2021 non-public offering of shares, Shandong Liyuan’s own funds, bank loans, or self-raised funds. The matter has been approved by the board of directors and still needs to be submitted to the shareholders’ meeting for approval.
