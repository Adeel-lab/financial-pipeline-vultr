General Motors (NYSE: GM) announced that Christopher T. Hatto, Vice President of Global Business Solutions and Chief Accounting Officer, has notified the company of his decision to retire, effective April 1, 2027.

According to a company statement, Hatto's retirement is not the result of any disagreement with the company regarding its operations, policies, or practices.

Hatto will continue to serve as the company's principal accounting officer through his retirement date to support an orderly transition. General Motors said it will announce the appointment of a successor principal accounting officer at a later date.
