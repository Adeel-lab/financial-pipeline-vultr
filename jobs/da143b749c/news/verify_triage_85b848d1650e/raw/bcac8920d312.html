Blue Ford Mustang GTD Suddenly Becomes a Street Monster, Albeit Only Across Imagination Land, Sadly

Czech Republic-based virtual artist Rostislav Prokop, known as "rostislav_prokop" on social media, continues his thunderous dream ride periplus with a Mustang
Published: 1 Nov 2026, 11:04 UTC
By:
Aurel Niculescu
  9 photos
Photo: rostislav_prokop / Instagram
As far as the Ford Motor Company is concerned, they would love nothing more than fully directing our attention toward the upcoming introduction of their all-new 2027 Ford Fathom, the ‘game-changing’ electric pickup that costs less than $30k. Alas, enthusiasts have other plans.
   

The Blue Oval has continued its teaser campaign for the upcoming 2027 Fathom with a recent post describing the truck’s bidirectional charging function, which is coming with 10 kW of power and some caveats. More importantly, though, the company has let slip the Fathom’s exact starting MSRP: $28,350.

On the other hand, folks want nothing to do with EVs and instead focus on things that have V8s under the hood – like the Ford Mustang Dark Horse or the recent rumor about a new hypercar featuring a 5.4-liter V8 redlining at 9,000 rpm. As it turns out, it’s not a Boss Mustang coming from around the corner, and instead the news is about Ford’s all-new Le Mans hypercar developed for the 2027 FIA World Endurance Championship (WEC) Hypercar program.

Anyway, even the members of the imaginative guild of digital car content creators have stopped dwelling across the parallel universes of vehicular CGI to take a look at the S650 Ford Mustang lately. For example, Czech Republic-based virtual artist Rostislav Prokop, known as "rostislav_prokop" on social media, continues his thunderous dream ride periplus with a Mustang GTD that would make even Ford blush a little.

His ‘street monster’ unofficial, hypothetical design project for the mighty S650 Ford Mustang GTD is “American muscle taken to the extreme,” with a vision for an ultra-widebody GTD aerodynamic kit, “a striking blue-and-red color combination, and aggressive street-focused proportions.” This version of the legal race car also rides on deep-dish, three-piece forged Rotiform wheels featuring carbon fiber lips — “a combination of precision, attitude, and pure performance-inspired design.”

The idea comes with things like RTR-inspired front grille LED elements, a lowered suspension setup, and new oval dual exhausts around the back – so it’s going to be quite easy to differentiate from the real Ford Mustang GTD. As for the powertrain, the pixel master doesn’t say anything about additional changes, meaning it’s the same 815-hp 5.2-liter supercharged V8.

By the way, the Ford Mustang GTD has been around since the summer of 2023 as a street-version of Blu Oval’s FIA Group GT3 race car and with the moniker an abbreviation of the Grand Touring Daytona class, which raced in the IMSA SportsCar Championship. The predator-equipped supercar was the first American car that lapped the infamous Nürburgring Nordschleife in less than seven minutes back in late 2024.

It beat its own record in May 2025, but in July 2025 General Motors quickly sent the 2026 Chevrolet Corvette ZR1 and ZR1X to beat its time, to which Ford CEO Jim Farley replied with “game on” after congratulating them. So, in April 2026, the Ford Mustang GTD once again went to the Green Hell, this time with the new Mustang GTD Competition version, and established the current reigning lap time of 6:40.835 for American cars.
