Weekly Recalls: Ford (2), Honda, Jaguar, Porsche **DO NOT DRIVE **, Ram Trucks, Subaru **DO NOT DRIVE**, Toyota **Fire Risk**, Volvo

Written By: CarPro | Oct 23, 2026, 10:56:54 AM

    Share:

**FORD (2)**

BLANK OR DISTORTED REARVIEW CAMERA IMAGE
CRASH RISK

  • #1-NHTSA ID Number: 26V650000
  • Manufacturer Ford Motor Company
  • Components BACK OVER PREVENTION
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 506

Summary

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2015 Transit Connect vehicles that were previously remedied under NHTSA recall number 25V572. The rearview camera may display a distorted, inverted, intermittent, or blank image when the vehicle is in reverse.

Remedy

Dealers will replace the rearview camera as necessary, free of charge. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, are expected to be mailed in October 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated in December 2027. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S73. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 7, 2026.

FRONT ROW SEATS IMPROPERLY SECURED

  • #2- NHTSA ID Number: 26V655000
  • Manufacturer Ford Motor Company
  • Components SEATS
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 2276

Summary

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025-2027 F-150 vehicles. The front seats may have been improperly secured to the vehicle.

Remedy

Dealers will inspect and tighten, or replace the front seat bolts as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 26, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S74. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 9, 2026.

**HONDA**

TIRE REPAIR KIT BOTTLE CAP MAY DETACH

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V663000
  • Manufacturer Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)
  • Components TIRES
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 4804

Summary

Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid, and 2025 CR-V Fuel Cell EV vehicles that were previously remedied under NHTSA recall number 26V366. When using the tire repair kit, pressure may build within the sealant bottle, causing the bottle cap to detach.

Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the tire repair kit nozzle, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 16, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is PPW. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 17, 2026. Vehicles previously repaired under recall 26V366 will need to have the new remedy completed.

**JAGUAR*

WATER MAY DAMAGE REARVIEW CAMERA
CRASH RISK

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V678000
  • Manufacturer Jaguar Land Rover North America, LLC
  • Components BACK OVER PREVENTION
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 93997

Summary

Jaguar Land Rover North America, LLC (Land Rover) is recalling certain 2022-2026 Land Rover Range Rover vehicles. Water may not be able to drain away from the rearview camera due to insufficient drain holes, which may result in damage to the rearview camera.

Remedy

Dealers will drill additional drain holes in the underside of the tailgate trim. Additionally, dealers will inspect and replace the rearview camera as necessary. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk are expected to be mailed December 11, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available. Owners may contact Land Rover's customer service at 800-637-6837. Land Rover's number for this recall is D171.

**PORSCHE**

DO NOT DRIVE WARNING
DO NOT DRIVE- CENTER LOCK MAY CAUSE TIRE DETACHMENT 

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V682000
  • Manufacturer Porsche Cars North America, Inc.
  • Components WHEELS
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 548

Summary

Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2024 718 Cayman GT4 RS, 911 Turbo S, 911 Turbo, 911 S/T, 911 GT3 RS, 911 GT3, 911 Carrera GTS, and 718 Spyder RS vehicles. The center lock wheel bolt may fracture and cause the wheel to detach.

Remedy

Owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until they have been repaired. Dealers will check the center lock wheel fastening system and replace any incorrectly manufactured parts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 15, 2026. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ATA1. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 24V809. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 20, 2026.

**RAM TRUCKS**

LOSS OF ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL
CRASH RISK

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V651000
  • Manufacturer Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)
  • Components ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC)
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 8329

Summary

Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2026 Ram 2500 Pickup trucks. The trucks may be equipped with steering column control modules that can cause a loss of electronic stability control. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems."

Remedy

Dealers will replace the steering column control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 24, 2026. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 90D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 13, 2026. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 26V244.

**SUBARU**

DO NOT DRIVE - LOSS OF VEHICLE CONTROL
DO NOT DRIVE WARNING

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V685000
  • Manufacturer Subaru of America, Inc.
  • Components SUSPENSION
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 8

Summary

Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2026 Crosstrek and Forester vehicles. The castle nut on the left-front lower control arm ball joint may be improperly fastened, which can lead to the ball joint separating from the lower control arm.

Remedy

Owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the remedy is completed. Dealers will inspect and replace the left-front lower control arm ball joint assembly, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 16, 2026. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRI26.

Click Here For NHTSA Documents

**TOYOTA**

FUEL TUBES MAY DISCONNECT AND LEAK
FIRE RISK

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V673000
  • Manufacturer Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing
  • Components FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 105794

Summary

Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024-2027 Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid vehicles. The fuel tube connection near the fuel tank may disconnect and leak fuel.

Remedy

Dealers will inspect the connection and install a supplemental clamp or, as necessary, replace the resin fuel tube and install a supplemental clamp, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 29, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26TB23 and 26TA23.

Click Here For NHTSA Documents

**VOLVO**

HEAD RESTRAINT CUSHION MAY DETACH

  • NHTSA ID Number: 26V671000
  • Manufacturer Volvo Car USA, LLC
  • Components SEATS
  • Potential Number of Units Affected: 9930

Summary

Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles. The head restraint cushion may detach during a crash.

Remedy

Dealers will apply an adhesive to reinforce the head cushion attachment, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 30, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Car’s customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo Car’s number for this recall is R10396. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 9, 2026.
