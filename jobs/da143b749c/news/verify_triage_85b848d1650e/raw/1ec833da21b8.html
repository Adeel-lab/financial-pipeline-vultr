Sheffield Lake announces $250,000 Ford Motor Company donation for new community playground
The Chronicle-Telegram
Published: Oct 29, 2026 7:06 PMUpdated: Oct 29, 2026 7:06 PM

Sheffield Lake announced Thursday that it received a $250,000 donation from Ford Motor Company that will fund a new playground at the Joyce Hanks Community Center at 4575 Lake Road.

The project will replace the current playground, creating a new recreational space for local children and families, a press release from the city says. The funds will cover the full cost of the project.

“We’re always looking for ways to make Sheffield Lake an even better place to live, work and raise a family," Mayor Rocky Radeff said in a news release. "This new playground will be a wonderful addition to the Joyce Hanks Community Center and a valuable resource for families throughout our community. We sincerely thank Ford Motor Company for its extraordinary generosity and commitment to helping our community thrive."

The Joyce Hanks Community Center serves as a hub in Sheffield Lake, hosting events for residents of all ages and offering rental space throughout the year, the release said. The new playground will enhance the center’s role as a gathering place for families and the broader community.

“At Ford, we believe in being an active and committed partner in the communities where our employees live and work. We understand the importance of giving back and supporting initiatives like this project in Sheffield Lake to help create a space where local families can gather, children can play, and the community can come together,” Gabby Bruno, director of community relations, Ford Motor Company, said in the release.

The city says securing the donation was the result of a collaborative, two-year effort.

After funding had been secured for a proposed field house project that ultimately wasn't approved by City Council, Encompass Marketing worked on behalf of the city to present the opportunity to Ford for a new community project, the release said. Six months after the revised proposal was submitted, Ford approved the funding for the playground.

“Bringing this project together took a great deal of time, hard work, and many conversations to find the right opportunity for this funding. Through persistence and collaboration with Ford Motor Company and the city of Sheffield Lake, we were able to turn this opportunity into something that will directly benefit the community,” Paula Pitasky, sponsorship coordinator for Sheffield Lake and president of Encompass Marketing, said in the release.
