Ford launches affordable EV chargers enabling its Fathom truck to power homes and grids.

Market News
27 Oct 2026, 17:51 WIB
·
TechCrunch
·View Source·
Bullish

Ford introduced two new EV chargers for its upcoming Fathom pickup, allowing the vehicle to supply power to homes or the grid. Priced at about half the cost of previous models, the chargers enable the Fathom, starting at $28,350, to deliver over 10 kW, enough to power essential appliances during outages. The Home Power Station helps reduce utility bills by charging during low rates and discharging during high rates, while the Home Backup Power package adds backup power capabilities. Both support virtual power plant participation, letting owners sell electricity back to the grid, potentially earning over $1,000 annually. This move highlights Ford's deeper push into energy storage amid rising demand for grid services.

Ford's new EV chargers for the Fathom pickup underscore its innovative approach to energy solutions, which may influence investor interest. On Pluang, Ford Motor Company (F) shares are priced at USD 11.78, up 1.68% as of Oct 27, 2026, 22:02 WIB. The stock carries a market cap of $46.18 billion and offers a dividend yield of 5.18%, reflecting steady investor confidence amid evolving automotive technologies.
