Ford CEO Says Its New EV Tech Is 30% More Efficient Than BYD's

Ford BYD Jim Farley electric vehicle

Ford has explicitly framed its Universal Electric Vehicle platform as a way to compete with Chinese cars on cost and technology. According to Ford CEO Jim Farley, the company has succeeded in at least one way: efficiency.

"There's another area that we really found to be quite advantaged relative to a BYD. And that is the efficiency of our motors, gearbox and inverters, which are about 30% more efficient than theirs and allows us to design a battery that's much less," Farley said today on the automaker's third-quarter earnings call. "I think that's really critical."
