Ford raises its annual Ebit forecast despite rising costs

Published on October 31, 2026 | Translated from Spanish

Ford beat analysts' expectations for the quarter with an adjusted operating profit of $2.7 billion and earnings per share of 49 cents versus the 40 expected, with revenue of $49.5 billion. The company raised the floor of its annual Ebit forecast by $500 million to $10.5 billion, although it kept the ceiling at $11 billion.

F-150 production and pressure on the supply chain 🏭

The manufacturer faces rising costs for copper, steel, fuel and chips, and raised its estimate of raw material cost overruns from $2 billion to almost $2.5 billion. F-150 production suffered disruptions due to a supplier, while the fire at Novelis is now almost resolved. The bottom line is that Ford is improving its numbers despite cost pressure and tariff uncertainty in North America.

Ford's secret formula: pay more for everything and still smile 😅

It turns out the recipe for earning more consists of spending more on copper, steel, fuel and chips, and along the way dealing with an F-150 supplier that takes vacations without warning. The almost-resolved fire at Novelis adds that touch of excitement that no business plan should include. Even so, Ford raises the floor of its forecast and keeps the ceiling: textbook prudence or simply that no one wants to overpromise with tariffs lurking.
