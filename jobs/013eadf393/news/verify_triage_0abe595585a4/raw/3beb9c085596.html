Ford said to target 20% sales growth by 2032

Business
Christian Baha / JJ
30 October

1 min read

EPA/CJ GUNTHER

Ford Motor Company aims for a 20% jump in sales by 2032, CEO Jim Farley told employees, the Wall Street Journal reported on Friday, citing people with knowledge of the matter.

According to the report, the executive said the carmaker plans to sell 5 million vehicles a year by early next decade. He also stated that the company will see sales rebound thanks to several new models set to be launched in the coming years, the sources said.

The people added that Farley stressed that manufacturing affordable vehicles remains a priority, and Ford intends to unveil five new models priced under $40,000 before the end of the 2020s, including an all-electric pickup truck. Earlier this week, the automaker revealed that its third-quarter revenue fell 2% year-over-year to $49.5 billion.
