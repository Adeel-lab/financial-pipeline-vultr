    China approves Geely acquisition of 34% of Ford España

    Published by Global Banking & Finance Review

    Posted on October 12, 2026

    1 min read

    · Last updated: October 14, 2026

    Add as preferred source on Google
    Finance Banking Markets

    Quick Summary

    China’s market regulator has unconditionally approved Geely’s acquisition of a 34% stake in Ford España, paving the way for a joint venture to produce electric and multi‑energy SUVs at Ford’s Valencia plant starting operations in early 2027.

    China Regulator Approves Geely's 34% Stake Acquisition in Ford España

    Geely's Strategic Investment in Ford España

    Regulatory Approval Details

    BEIJING, Oct 12 (Reuters) - China's market regulator has unconditionally approved Geely's acquisition of 34% of Ford Motor's Spanish subsidiary Ford España, it said on Monday.

    Joint Venture and Production Plans

    Electric SUV Manufacturing in Valencia

    In July the companies said Geely would build two electric SUVs at Ford’s factory in Valencia, Spain, and they would together develop a vehicle under a newly formed joint venture.

    Ownership Structure of the Joint Venture

    The JV is to be 66% owned by Ford and 34% owned by Geely, the companies added. 

    Reporting and Editorial Credits

    (Reporting by Beijing Newsroom; Editing by Louise Heavens and David Dolan)

    Key Takeaways

    • Regulatory approval by China’s SAMR clears the way for Geely to take a 34% stake in Ford’s Spanish subsidiary—Ford España—without conditions (europapress.es).
    • The upcoming joint venture, with 66% owned by Ford and 34% by Geely, will transform Ford’s Valencia plant into a high‑tech shared manufacturing hub, producing multiple Ford and Geely models—including electric SUVs—from 2028 (global.geely.com).
    • Geely invested approximately €221 million for its 34% stake, valuing the new joint venture at about €650 million, reinforcing its European expansion and securing long‑term industrial stability for the Valencia facility (efe.com)
